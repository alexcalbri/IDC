# CRM Module

Status: in development (`.development`).

Lead management on top of the shared customer identity. A lead is the
commercial side of a customer from Clientes: the person is never duplicated and
can later take part in other modules. The design follows the lead boards used
by tourism businesses (one board per line of business with stages such as New,
Answered, In communication, Booked, Cold, Old guest) and real-estate CRMs
(pipeline, activity timeline, stage history).

## Concepts

- **Lead** (`crm_leads`): customer, stage, optional interest, responsible user,
  source, language, original message, working details, dates the lead is
  interested in (booking, visit, move-in), estimated value and next follow-up.
- **Customer code**: the `customer_code` remains owned and generated by Clientes;
  CRM displays it read-only on the lead detail and never uses the database ID as
  the guest-facing code.
- **Stages** (`crm_stages`): configurable pipeline. Each stage has a kind
  (`open`, `won`, `lost`), a tone (color meaning drawn by the app theme) and a
  position. Entering a won or lost stage sets `closed_at`; reopening clears it.
  New companies start with *Nuevo, Contactado, En seguimiento, Ganado,
  Perdido*. While a company has no leads it can switch to a template:
  *General*, *Turismo y hospedaje* or *Bienes raices*.
- **Activity** (`crm_activity`): notes and every change (creation, stage,
  responsible, interest, data), including the stage history (`from_stage_id`,
  `to_stage_id`).

## Interests (optional, per company)

Off by default. The company turns it on in *Configuracion* when leads are
split by line of business (Surf, Yoga, Eventos...) or by property type.

- The company defines its interests (`crm_interests`) and assigns interests to
  each user (`crm_user_interests`).
- A user sees the leads of their interests and the leads they are assigned to (0.19.0: even when the lead is outside their interests; see *Assigned users*).
- Users with **Ver todos los leads** (and the business owner) see every lead
  and use the interest as a filter.
- Leads without interest and without assigned users wait in **Sin
  calificar**, visible to users with **Recepcion de leads** (reception is a
  role), who categorize them.
- Deleting an interest sends its leads back to Recepcion.
- With interests off, every CRM user sees every lead and no interest is shown.

The visibility rule lives in `CrmScope` (`canSee` and its SQL twin
`sqlCondition`) and is applied by every lead query.

## Assigned users (V017, 0.19.0)

A lead can be assigned to several users, all equal (there is no main person). The table
`crm_lead_owners` (`lead_id`, `user_id`, `created_at`; primary key `(lead_id, user_id)`; lead rows are deleted
with the lead; index by `user_id`) is the **only** source of truth: no query or write of the module uses
`crm_leads.owner_user_id` any more. V017 copied the previous assignee of every lead into the table and kept the
column (and its index) only so the change can be rolled back; it is deprecated and no longer updated, and a
later migration can drop it. `CrmAssignees` holds the SQL fragments and the rules without a database.

- **Visibility:** with interests on, each assigned user sees the lead even outside their interests
  (`CrmScope.canSee` / `sqlCondition`, `EXISTS` over `crm_lead_owners`).
- **Mis leads / Asignado filter:** leads where the user is one of the assigned; **Sin asignar** = nobody assigned.
- **Sin calificar (rule changed from 0.18.0):** until 0.18.0 a lead stayed in the list while it lacked an interest
  **or** an assigned user, and left only when it had both. Since 0.19.0 a lead is in the list only while it has
  **no assigned user and no interest** (with interests off: no assigned user), and leaves as soon as it has at least
  one assigned user **or** an interest (`unqualifiedSql`). Leads that had one of the two and were listed before no longer are.
- **Lead form:** *Asignado* is a multiple dropdown (`UiField.multiple`). The form carries the list it was drawn with
  (request parameter `owners0`) and saving applies only the difference to it (adds with add, removes with remove), so
  assignments made by somebody else meanwhile are not undone; the notification goes to the users really added.
  Without permission to assign (or when an agent sends no `owner`/`owners`) the assigned users are not touched at all.
  Assigned users that can no longer have leads (deactivated) show as "<usuario> (sin acceso)" so they can be unchecked,
  but they cannot be added; they also can be removed with bulk "Quitar asignado" and `ownersMode=remove`. They are never
  shown in the `{responsable}` template variable. Cards and list rows show the initials of the assigned users.
- **Suggestions:** approving adds the suggested user; if they no longer have access to the CRM nobody is assigned,
  the suggestion is only discarded and the user is told.
- **Concurrency:** changes of the assigned users lock the lead row (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`) and use set statements
  (`INSERT ... SELECT unnest(...) ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING RETURNING`, `DELETE ... RETURNING`), so what was really
  added or removed is known and notified.
- **Notifications:** each user added (form, bulk, agents, approved suggestion) gets "Te asignaron un lead" in the
  Core notification center (`ModuleNotifier`, target screen `lead` with its id) and, if `notify_assigned` is on in
  `crm_user_settings`, the email too (failures are logged without personal data and never fail the change). Nobody is notified of assigning themselves. Imports add assigned users silently.
- **Follow-ups and daily digest** reach every assigned user; the template variable `{responsable}` lists all names.
- **History:** "Asignados: ..." entries (kind `owner`) say who the lead has now.
- **Imports:** the people column accepts several people (monday writes "Ana, Beto"; CSV accepts comma or
  semicolon). Each person is matched with a user by the equivalence table of the mapping, then by profile email,
  then by name. People without a user are skipped and listed in the row report ("Sin usuario para asignar"); no user
  is ever created. Importing again **only adds** assigned users and never removes any (users assigned by hand or removed
  later are not touched), and it never repeats the ones the lead already has (`ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`).
  In monday and CSV the cell is split at every comma or semicolon, so a name written as "Perez, Ana" becomes two
  people ("Perez" and "Ana"): write it as "Ana Perez" or map each piece in the equivalence table.
- **WordPress, Encuentra24, Cloudbeds:** they do not assign directly; the agent of a listing is a *suggestion*
  (`suggested_owner_user_id`) and approving it adds the user to the list.

## Mentions in notes (V018, 0.20.0)

A note of a lead can mention any user with access to the CRM (active, business owner or holder of `crm.view`),
even if the lead is not theirs or of their interests. Two ways to mention, because the app has no "@" autocomplete
in text fields yet:

- the multiple dropdown **Mencionar a** next to the note (`UiField.multiple`, as *Asignado*);
- `@Nombre` or `@correo` in the text of the note, matched against the same users (`CrmMentions.parseText`): names may
  have spaces ("@Beto Ruiz", the longest match wins), case is ignored, an `@` glued to a word is an email address and not a
  mention, repeated mentions count once and an unknown `@word` is reported ("No se reconocio: @Zoe") without failing the note.

Table `crm_lead_mentions` (V018, in `runtimeTables`): `id`, `lead_id` (FK `crm_leads`, `ON DELETE CASCADE`), `user_id`,
`activity_id` (the note in `crm_activity`; `ON DELETE SET NULL`), `mentioned_by`, `created_at` and `revoked_at`.
Partial index by `user_id WHERE revoked_at IS NULL`; unique `(activity_id, user_id)`, so one note never mentions (or
notifies) a user twice, even revoked. V018 also adds `crm_user_settings.notify_mentioned` (default true).

**What a mention gives.** While the mention is active (`revoked_at IS NULL`) the user sees the lead in **Leads** (pipeline
and list) with the badge **Mencionado**, can open it, read its data, notes, conversation and files, and **add notes**: nothing
else. An archived lead disappears from their views (also with **Mostrar archivados**). Visibility has two kinds and they are never mixed:

| Kind | Where | Allows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| By scope (`CrmScope.canSee`, `sqlCondition()`) | interests, assigned, reception, see all | everything their permissions allow |
| By mention (`CrmScope.canRead`, `sqlCondition(withMentions = true)`, `CrmMentions.LeadAccess.ReadNotes`) | `EXISTS` over active `crm_lead_mentions` | read the lead and add notes |

Only the lead views (`CrmLeadFilter.withMentions`: pipeline, list) and the agent search use the mention-aware condition;
every write (`lead:save`, `restore`, `send`, `delete`, `approve`/`discard`, items, rooms, `pipeline:move`, `unmention`, bulk
actions, the agent tools) keeps asking `canSee`. The note form is the only write open to a mention (`lead:note`; the user also
needs `crm.view`). Bulk actions skip the leads seen only through a mention, in the screen (they get no checkbox) and again inside
`CrmBulkActions` ("solo los ves por una mencion").

**Who gives access.** Only a user with the lead inside their normal scope (`LeadAccess.Full`) creates mentions. Someone who
reads through a mention can write notes, but their `@` stay plain text: no access is created, no revoked mention comes back and
nobody is told, in the app and in `crm_add_lead_note` (`mentionsIgnored`). So "Quitar acceso" is final unless someone who manages the lead
mentions the user again. A mention reads only while the lead is open: an archived lead is not reached through it (list SQL and direct open),
and the mentioned user sees only the lead data, the conversation, the notes and the activity (no customer notes, items, reservations or suggestions).
A name followed by `. _ + -` and a letter or digit is not that user ("@Ana-Maria" is not "@Ana"); runs of spaces/NBSP count as one.

**Quitar acceso.** Whoever manages the lead (`crm.leads.manage` with the lead inside their scope) sees the list **Mencionados**
in the lead with a **Quitar acceso** button per user (`lead:unmention`): it sets `revoked_at` on the user's active mentions and
records it in the history. A later note that mentions them gives access again. Notes that mention someone are highlighted in the
history (tone and the title "Nota del lead - menciona a @Ana"; the renderer has no inline styles).

**Notices.** Each newly mentioned user gets "Te mencionaron en un lead" in the Core notification center (target screen `lead`
with its id; the text is the lead, who wrote and the note cut to 140 characters) and, if `notify_mentioned` is on and they have a
profile email, an email to that email through `crm_outbox` (kind `mentioned`). Nobody is told for mentioning themselves or twice for
the same note. **Equipo** has the per-user option "Avisarme por correo cuando me mencionen" (`crm_update_user_notifications` has `notifyMentioned`).

## "@" autocomplete and pipeline selection (0.21.0)

**Mention autocomplete.** The note field of the lead carries `UiSuggest(route, minLength = 0, trigger = "@")`. The route is
`GET /modules/crm/ui/suggest/mentions?lead=<id>&q=<text after @>`: up to 8 mentionable users (the same set as the "Mencionar a"
dropdown, never the writer) whose name contains `q` (ignoring case; emails are never matched); with an empty `q`, the first 8 alphabetically. Each
`UiSuggestion` has the name as title and `values.insert` = the name (names with spaces resolve in `CrmMentions.parseText`); the
subtitle is empty unless two suggested users share a name, then it is their email. Only who can give access by mentioning gets
suggestions (crm.leads.manage with the lead inside their scope); a mention-only reader gets an empty list. The "Mencionar a" dropdown stays for old clients and the MCP.

**Pipeline actions.** `UiBoard.actions` declares what the app runs on selected cards (or one card): move (options = stages),
assign me, assign to (options = users), remove assigned, archive, restore (only with the archived column shown) and delete
(with confirmation). Each is a `UiRequest` to `pipeline` with `op=action`, `action=<id>`; the app adds `cards` (comma ids, up to 500)
and `value`. The server runs them through `CrmBulkActions`, so scope and permissions are the bulk bar's: assign actions need the
assign permission (or seeing every lead), delete needs crm.leads.delete, leads seen only by a mention are skipped, and move and
restore only touch open and archived leads respectively. Only the actions the user may run are declared; any other id is refused.
The reply is the refreshed pipeline with the summary ("N lead(s) ...") and how many cards were rejected. The old `pipeline` and `bulk` routes are unchanged.

## Inbox and channels (V002)

Every channel (forms, email, and the channel integrations) hands contacts to
`CrmInbox.receive`:

1. Email typos are fixed (`gmial.com`, `hotmail.con`...) and the phone is
   normalized to `+<digits>` (8 local digits get +505, 10 digits shaped like
   USA/Canada get +1, `00` becomes `+`).
2. The person is found by channel identity (`crm_identities`, e.g. a Messenger
   PSID), then email, then phone. When email and phone belong to different
   customers the lead is linked by email and flagged **Por revisar**; people
   are never merged automatically.
3. Anti-spam rules (`crm_spam_settings`) run for the channels they are enabled
   on, except for contacts that already have an open lead (optional). Blocked
   messages create nothing and are kept in the last-50 log.
4. The message joins the customer's open lead, or a new lead is created in the
   first open stage with its origin (`source`, e.g. "WhatsApp") and, when the
   source suggests one, an assigned user waiting in **Sin calificar** (`suggested_owner_user_id`;
   approving it adds the person to the assigned users). Nothing is assigned automatically.

The lead detail shows the **Conversacion** (`crm_messages`, deduplicated by the
channel's message id) and replies through any available channel (`CrmChannels`;
today email).

## Email

- **Sending** (`crm_mail_settings`): the server SMTP or the company's own
  account. Replies and queued mail use it.
- **Templates** (`crm_email_templates`): subject and HTML or plain-text body
  with placeholders `{nombre}`, `{correo}`, `{telefono}`, `{codigo_cliente}`
  (the customer code, also the WiFi code of the hotspot), `{responsable}` (every assigned user, names separated by comma),
  `{etapa}`, `{interes}`, `{checkin}`, `{checkout}`, `{valor}`, `{habitaciones}`,
  `{reserva}`. Values are HTML-escaped.
- **Reading** (IMAP, optional): job `imap` every 5 minutes reads up to 50 new
  messages of the configured folder (read-only; the first read starts from the
  newest message) and passes them to the inbox with the quoted history cut.

## Notifications

Users have no email in the core, so **Equipo** stores one per user
(`crm_user_settings`) with four options (the fourth, 0.20.0: email when they are mentioned, to their profile email): every new lead (only users that see
all leads), leads assigned to them, and a daily digest of their follow-ups due (every assigned user gets them)
(at the company's digest hour, once a day). Mail goes through `crm_outbox`,
sent by job `outbox` every minute with up to 5 attempts.

## Meta: WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram (V003, V013)

**Configuracion > Integraciones > Meta Business** with one Meta app per company
(each company's own app and Meta Business portfolio). The screen follows the
WordPress plugin (CT Leads Extension 4.57):

1. **Guia paso a paso** (accordion, open while Meta is off): business portfolio,
   app and permissions, system user, webhooks and this screen.
2. **Configuracion**: the callback URL and the common settings, then one
   accordion per channel (`UiForm.collapsibleSections`, each with its state in
   `sectionNotes`):
   - Common: **App Secret** (validates `X-Hub-Signature-256` of every call of
     the three channels), **verify token**, **system user token** of Meta
     Business (needs `whatsapp_business_messaging`, `whatsapp_business_management`
     and, when shared, `pages_messaging`, `pages_show_list`,
     `pages_manage_metadata`, `pages_read_engagement`, `instagram_basic`,
     `instagram_manage_messages`) and **shared tokens**. Secrets are masked.
   - **WhatsApp**: WhatsApp Business Account ID, Phone Number ID and
     coexistence echoes.
   - **Messenger**: the connected page (read only), the page ID when the token
     manages several pages, or its own page token.
   - **Instagram**: the linked account (read only) or its own token.
3. **Registro de eventos** (accordion, closed): the last 30 events.

Messenger and Instagram always work through the page (Facebook login): the
Instagram route with Instagram login (`graph.instagram.com` and its own app
secret) is not supported. With **shared tokens** (default) both reply with the
page token read from the system token (`GET /me/accounts`, it does not expire)
and the Instagram account linked to the page is taken from it. Off, each uses a
page token of its own; the Instagram account is read from the page of the
Instagram token (`GET /me?fields=instagram_business_account`), never typed.

**Guardar y conectar** is the only button and does every connection at once,
each step reported and logged on its own: page token and Instagram account,
Instagram subscription (`POST /{ig}/subscribed_apps?subscribed_fields=messages`;
the callback URL alone does not subscribe Instagram) and the WhatsApp Business
Account (`POST /{waba}/subscribed_apps`, numbers with their platform; one number
is saved, several are offered to pick). The agent tools `meta_connect_page`,
`meta_connect_whatsapp` and `meta_fill_names` remain for agents.

Callback URL (one for all): `https://<server>/modules/crm/hooks/<company>/meta`.
WhatsApp fields `messages` and `smb_message_echoes`; page fields `messages`
and `standby`; Instagram `messages`.

### Server app (V014): connect by logging in

Instead of each company creating and reviewing its own Meta app, the server
owner can offer one app to every company of the server, like the CRM services
that only ask to log in:

- **Server owner** (server workspace, CRM active there): Integraciones shows
  **App de Meta del servidor** with a guide and App ID, App Secret, verify
  token and the two Facebook Login for Business configuration ids (Messenger
  and Instagram; WhatsApp Embedded Signup). The owner's business portfolio is
  verified, verified as Tech Provider, and the app goes through App Review once.
  The app uses one callback URL for the whole server:
  `https://<server>/modules/crm/meta/webhook`, plus
  `/modules/crm/meta/deauthorize` and `/modules/crm/meta/data-deletion`.
- **Company**: **Usar la app del servidor** (`connection_mode = 'server'`) and
  **Conectar con Meta** create a 30-minute link (`crm_meta_tickets`, hashed)
  that opens `/modules/crm/meta/connect/<company>` in the browser. The page
  uses the JS SDK of the server app: Facebook login gives a token whose pages
  are listed (one is connected directly, several are offered to choose), the
  page is subscribed (`messages`, `standby`) with its Instagram account; WhatsApp
  Embedded Signup gives the business token, the WhatsApp Business Account and
  the number, and the app subscribes to the account. Numbers already on the
  Cloud API (coexistence included, or used by another system such as the
  WordPress plugin) are never registered again; a new number is registered
  with the two-step PIN typed on the page. **Desconectar** forgets the tokens.
- **Routing** (`crm_meta_routes`, in the server workspace): page, Instagram
  account and WhatsApp Business Account of each company, unique per asset, and
  the Meta user who connected. The server webhook checks the signature with the
  server App Secret, splits the entries by company and hands each part to the
  company as if it came to its own URL. Deauthorize and data deletion
  disconnect the companies that person connected.
- Messages, names, replies and errors work as with the company's own app. The
  agent tools that change Meta settings only apply to companies with their own app.

Incoming: WhatsApp messages create or update the lead by phone (`+wa_id`,
profile name); Messenger / Instagram by PSID / IGSID identity. Their name is
asked to Meta only while the contact has none: first from the participants of
the conversation (`/me/conversations`, needs only the messaging permission),
then from the profile; a contact that wrote before Meta gave its name gets it
on its next message, and the agent tool `meta_fill_names` fills the contacts
still named "Contacto de Messenger/Instagram ..." (100 per channel, never
replacing a name; failures logged as `nombre_error`). Media, locations and
contacts are stored as readable text; reactions, page echoes, deleted messages
and the company's own Instagram account are ignored; repeated deliveries are
deduplicated by message id. Coexistence echoes (replies from the WhatsApp
Business app) are stored as outgoing messages of the open lead and never create
leads. The answer to Meta is 200 even when one message fails (logged); the last
30 events are shown.

Outgoing: WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram appear as reply channels on the
lead. WhatsApp errors are explained (131047: outside the 24-hour window, only
approved templates; 131026 / 131030: number cannot receive; 190: token
expired). Messenger / Instagram split long texts (2000 / 1000 characters), take
Messenger thread control once when the Business Suite inbox controls the
conversation, and explain the 24-hour window (subcodes 2018278 / 2534022),
unavailable people (551), expired tokens (190) and missing permissions (10, 200,
230), saying when the first parts were already sent. Send errors are logged.

## Encuentra24 (V004)

**Integraciones > Encuentra24**: API key and company slug. Job `encuentra24`
every 5 minutes calls the Leads API (`/api.php/v1/crm/index`, `x-api-key`) from
one day before the last sync (7 days the first time), 200 per page following
`pagination.nextid`, up to 25 pages, oldest first. Each message, chat or call
goes to the inbox with channel `encuentra24`, origin "Encuentra24", the listing
title and id, the chat link and the original date; the Encuentra24 lead id is
the message id, so nothing is imported twice. Contacts without email and phone
are skipped. The listing (`adid`, title) joins the lead's interests as an item
with source `encuentra24`. **Importar ahora** imports the history from a chosen
date; an advisory lock keeps it and the job from running together. The first
error of a failed lead is kept in the last result. The API cannot reply or
change states in Encuentra24.

Listing links: a message of any other channel (WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram,
email, form) with a link to an encuentra24.com listing (its WhatsApp button
adds it) gives a new lead the origin "Encuentra24 (<channel>)" and links the
listing to the lead (by its numeric id, or by the link when it has none),

## WordPress / Real Estate 7 (V005)

Integration through the **IDC Connector** plugin
(`server/src/main/resources/modules/crm/wordpress/idc-connector/`), downloaded
from **Integraciones > WordPress** (`/modules/crm/wordpress/idc-connector.zip`,
built from the module resources). A plugin is used instead of database access:
MySQL is rarely reachable from outside the hosting, would give IDC the whole
WordPress database, and Real Estate 7 data (postmeta `_ct_*`, 11 taxonomies,
serialized values) and its forms only exist inside WordPress.

- **Security**: the CRM generates a secret (48 characters) that is pasted in the
  plugin. Both directions send `X-IDC-Timestamp` and
  `X-IDC-Signature = hex(HMAC-SHA256(secret, timestamp + "." + body))`;
  more than 5 minutes of difference is rejected.
- **IDC -> site** (`POST /wp-json/idc/v1/rpc`): `ping`, `search` (text or MLS,
  taxonomy filters, price range, 20 per page), `listing` (every field of the
  theme: price, MLS, size, lot, year, parking, taxonomies, agent, co-agent,
  brokerage, open house, rental fields, video, virtual tour, gallery and all the
  `_ct_*` meta), `taxonomies`, `agents`, `sold` (status Sold, `_ct_sold_date`,
  `_idc_sold_price`).
- **Site -> IDC** (`POST /modules/crm/hooks/<company>/wordpress`): CT Forms
  submissions (`ct_forms_after_send`, listing detected from the page), listing
  inquiries (`ajax-submit-listings`) and showing requests
  (`ajax-submit-book-listing-showing`) create or update leads (channel `form`,
  origin "Formulario - <property>") with the property linked and its agent as
  suggested responsible (agents mapped to IDC users in Integraciones).
  Favorites (`wpfp_favorites`), saved searches with alerts and property views
  of signed-in users go to the activity of the open lead and never create
  leads. Failed events are queued in WordPress and retried hourly.
- **reCAPTCHA v3** (optional, in the plugin): verified on the server before CT
  Forms handles the submission.

**Lead items** (`crm_lead_items`): properties of the site (`wordpress`), rooms
of a PMS (next version) or items typed by hand (`manual`), with status
Interesado, Ofrecida, Elegida, Descartada, Vendida or Reservada. The lead
detail searches the site (**Buscar propiedades**), links several properties and
**Cerrar venta** marks the property sold on the site and moves the lead to the
first won stage.

## Archive after check-out and archived leads (V015, 0.17.0)

**Automatizacion de salida** (Configuracion, `crm.settings`) is stored in `crm_settings`
(`exit_enabled`, `exit_hour` default 14, `exit_time_zone`, `exit_stage_ids`). It only touches leads
in the chosen origin stages. The rules are pure functions in `CrmExit` (tested in `CrmExitTest`):

- **Without active Cloudbeds** the job `exit-archive` (15 min) archives the open leads whose end date
  (`target_end`, the check-out) is before today (at once) or today once the configured hour has passed,
  in the company's time zone. It is one atomic `UPDATE ... RETURNING` that repeats the conditions, with
  the activity "Archivado automaticamente al hacer check-out." of each lead in the same transaction;
  archived leads are never selected again (idempotent). The configuration is read on every run.
- **With active Cloudbeds** the hour never archives. The job archives the leads of the chosen stages whose
  reservation is `checked_out` (and has no open reservation), which also covers leads that reached the
  stage after the check-out or were already checked out before the automation was turned on; `cloudbeds-sync`
  archives at the moment of the check-out. Both need "archivar al hacer check-out" on in Integraciones; with
  it off nothing is archived (the card says so). A lead without a Cloudbeds reservation is never archived.
- **Turning it on** archives every lead already past its check-out, so the card shows how many per stage
  and asks for a confirmation (`exitConfirm`) before saving; the agent tool needs `confirmArchiveOverdue`.
- **Stages:** deleting a stage removes it from `exit_stage_ids` (the automation turns off if none is left),
  applying a stage template resets it, and `CrmExit.load` ignores ids of stages that no longer exist.
- **Restaurar** (`lead:restore`, `crm.leads.manage` and the lead inside the user's scope): sets `archived_at` to NULL
  in a transaction, keeps the stage and `closed_at`, and leaves the activity "Lead restaurado."; an open lead is
  left alone (a notice says so). Buttons: the lead detail (instead of moving) and a "Restaurar archivados" card
  under the pipeline and the list (cards and rows only open the lead). Agents: `crm_restore_lead`. A restored lead
  (`crm_leads.restored_at`, V015) stays out of the exit automation, by hour and by Cloudbeds check-out, until its
  stage (`moveLead`) or end date (`updateLead`) changes; imports changing the date do not clear it. Neither
  Cloudbeds nor imports restore: a lead archived at check-out with a new open reservation stays archived.
- **Archived leads do not move:** `CrmRepository.moveLead` refuses them ("Restaura el lead antes de moverlo"),
  which covers the lead form, bulk actions and `crm_move_lead`/`crm_update_lead` (use Restaurar first).
- **Time zone:** the project has no time zone per company, so the card has its own IANA zone
  (empty uses the server's, as the daily digest does).

**Mostrar archivados** (pipeline and leads): a checkbox in the filters, remembered per user in
`crm_user_settings.show_archived`. `CrmLeadFilter.archived` (only archived, the pipeline column) and
`includeArchived` (mixed in, the list) feed `archivedSql`; by default archived leads stay hidden.
Bulk actions, follow-ups and Sin calificar never show them. The Archivados column lists the most
recently archived first. V015 adds the partial indexes `crm_leads_exit_due_idx` and `crm_leads_archived_idx`.

## Cloudbeds (V006)

**Integraciones > Cloudbeds**: API key of the property (scopes `read:room`,
`read:rate`, `read:reservation`, `write:reservation`), optional property ID,
the payment method sent to Cloudbeds (required by `postReservation`; payments
are handled in Cloudbeds), welcome email (template, days before check-in,
hour) and archiving after check-out.

- **Buscar habitaciones** (lead detail): free rooms for the lead's dates and
  guests: `getRooms` with dates (unassigned rooms; blocked and virtual ones
  dropped) joined with the cheapest available `totalRate` of each type from
  `getRatePlans`. A user assigns one or several rooms (`crm_lead_items`, source
  `cloudbeds`, status Elegida); the rooms of one reservation share dates.
- **Confirmar reserva**: guests per room, optional country (2 letters) and zip;
  the lead's email is required by Cloudbeds. Availability is checked again,
  `postReservation` creates one reservation with every room (`roomID` per
  room, `sendEmailConfirmation=false`, `thirdPartyIdentifier=idc-<lead>`),
  rooms become Reservada, the reservation is stored (`crm_reservations`) and
  the lead moves to the first won stage. Rooms left unassigned by Cloudbeds are
  reported. A lead with assigned rooms cannot be dragged to a won stage.
- **Welcome email** (job `cloudbeds-welcome`, 15 min): from the configured hour,
  reservations whose check-in is the configured days away or less get the
  welcome template (with `{codigo_cliente}` as WiFi password, `{checkin}`,
  `{checkout}`, `{habitaciones}`, `{reserva}`) once, through the outbox; it is
  also added to the conversation.
- **Status** (job `cloudbeds-sync`, 30 min): `getReservation` of open
  reservations; changes go to the activity and `checked_out` archives the lead
  (`crm_leads.archived_at`): archived leads leave the pipeline, lists and counts
  unless **Mostrar archivados** is on (with the exit automation on, only leads in its stages).

## Imports and bulk actions (V007)

**Configuracion > Importar** reads rows from:

- **monday** (GraphQL API with a personal token): the token is saved for the
  company (`crm_settings.monday_token`, V008) and the active boards it can read
  are listed by workspace, each with a checkbox (subitem boards and docs are
  left out). For every marked board: every column as text with its monday
  type, the group, the board (mapped to the interest), the creation date,
  updates (up to 100 per item) with their replies and subitems as notes, and
  the files of file columns and of updates; the item id is the import key. Reading a big board takes minutes,
  longer than the app waits for a response, so marking boards only queues the
  import (status `reading`, V009); the `import` job reads them on its next
  minute and leaves the import ready to map, or `failed` with the reason.
- **CT Leads Pro** through the IDC Connector plugin (0.2.0, action `leads`):
  name, email, phone, pipeline stage, assigned agent, source, tags, notes,
  messages and interested listings (linked as properties).
- **Files**: a CSV pasted as text or a CSV/XLSX from a public https URL (private
  addresses refused, 20 MB, 20,000 rows; XLSX without DTDs). CSV with comma,
  semicolon or tab, BOM and multiline cells. The monday Excel export layout
  (title, one line per group, repeated header) is recognized and the group goes
  to a "Grupo" column.

Each import (`crm_import_jobs`, rows in `crm_import_rows`) goes through:
**mapping** of every column to a CRM field (guessed from the header; monday and
Leads Pro names are known) and of the stage, owner and interest values to the
CRM ones (saved mappings in `crm_import_presets`); **preview** (new, updated,
to review, skipped, without writing); and the **import**, 300 rows a minute by
job `import`, each row committed on its own, cancelable.

Rules (from the CT Leads Extension importer): email typos fixed and phones
normalized; the person is matched by email or phone; when the email and the
phone belong to different customers the row stays **por revisar** and nothing
is merged; rows without name, email and phone are skipped; importing again
updates the lead with the same import key (`crm_leads.import_key`); historical
comments become notes with their original date and the same note is never
added twice; imports send no notifications.

**Fields and files (V012)**: besides the lead fields, a column can go to the
lead's files (`files`), to an existing custom field of the CRM (`crm:<key>`) or
of the customer in Clientes (`cliente:<key>`, values in
`customers.flexible_attributes`), or to a field created when the mapping is
saved (`new:crm`, `new:cliente`). Columns without a field are proposed as an
existing field with the same name, the lead's files for monday file columns,
a new customer field for personal data (document id, birth date, address...)
and a new CRM field for anything else with values; the mapping screen lists
them under **Campos que faltan** so the user confirms or picks "No importar".
New fields take the type of the monday column (status and dropdown become
lists with the values of the rows; dropdown allows several). Invalid values
are left out and reported with the row; empty values never erase.
Files are kept as monday asset ids with the row and downloaded when the row is
applied (monday links expire within the hour): column files go to
Documentos in a folder named after the column (or to the mapped CRM files
field), update files to Documentos > Actualizaciones de monday > date; each
file is attached once per lead (`crm_attachments.source_ref`); files over 25 MB
or that fail are reported with the row. The `import` job stops after about 50
seconds per run so large downloads continue on the next minute.

**Acciones en lote** (0.18.0, no longer a section): an accordion in the Lista view of
Leads, only with `crm.leads.manage`. One checkbox per lead of the current page,
"select all visible", and move to a stage, **Anadir asignado**, **Quitar asignado** or **Sin asignar** (with
`crm.assign` or see-all; 0.19.0, they replaced "Asignar responsable"), archive, restore or delete (with `crm.leads.delete`).
A lead that would not change (already assigned, not assigned) is skipped and counted, so repeating the action is harmless.
The renderer has no row selection, so it is a form with one checkbox field per lead.

## Custom fields and attachments (V010)

- **Custom fields** (`crm_field_definitions`): created in Configuracion with a name, a type
  (Texto, Texto largo, Numero, Correo, Telefono, Enlace, Fecha, Si / No, Lista desplegable,
  Archivos y carpetas), whether it is required and its position. A dropdown has its own options
  (one per line) and may allow several (`multiple`, sent to the app as `UiField.multiple`). The
  key is `cf_` plus the name (`cf_fecha_de_firma`) and the type never changes after creation.
  Values live in `crm_leads.custom_fields` (JSONB: strings, booleans, arrays for multiple
  dropdowns); an option removed from the list stays valid in the leads that already had it.
  Changes are logged in the lead history. Deleting a field deletes its values and files.
- **Attachments** (`crm_attachments`): every lead has a built-in **Documentos** field and custom
  fields of type Archivos work the same way. Files or whole folders (with subfolders, kept as
  `folder_path`) are uploaded one by one to `POST /modules/crm/ui/files` (`crm.leads.manage`,
  25 MB per file, 500 files per lead) and streamed to the server file storage of the company
  (`ModuleAccessService.files`, under `COMPANY_FILES_ROOT`); the row keeps name, folder, lead,
  `storage_backend` and `storage_ref` (V011). An upload belongs to no lead until the form is
  saved. Removing a file, deleting its field or deleting the lead only marks the row; the
  `attachments-cleanup` job deletes the stored file and then the row, so a failed save never
  loses a file. The same job moves files still kept in the database by V010.
  `GET /modules/crm/ui/files/{id}` downloads a file of a lead the user can see. Added and
  removed files are logged in the history (kind `files`).
- Agents read the definitions in `crm_list_pipeline`, the values in `crm_get_lead` and set them
  with `customFields` in `crm_create_lead` / `crm_update_lead` (by name or key); files are only
  attached from the app.

## Field sections and stay dates (V019, 0.22.0)

- **Sections** (`crm_field_sections`: id, name, position; `crm_field_definitions.section_id`, nullable). In *Configuracion > General* the
  company creates, renames, orders and deletes sections and picks the section of each custom field. The lead form and detail draw
  each section with its fields (sections by position, fields by position inside; `CrmFieldSections.ordered`), and the fields without a
  section go last in **Campos personalizados**. Deleting a section keeps its fields (they become unsectioned). The fixed section
  "Lo que busca" is gone; **Valor estimado** is a fixed field of **Seguimiento**. Names of the fixed sections cannot be reused.
- **Stay dates are custom fields.** `crm_settings.stay_start_field` / `stay_end_field` hold the keys of the date fields that count as
  check-in and check-out, chosen in *Configuracion > Integraciones > Fechas de estadia* (also shown in the Cloudbeds block). `crm_leads.target_start`
  and `target_end` stay as an **internal copy** that the repository fills from the mapped fields on every save of a lead (form, MCP,
  import, `CrmStay.resync`) and that is empty without mapping; the hotspot (`HotspotCrmPipeline`), the exit automation (`CrmExit`),
  `restoreSurvives`, the Cloudbeds default dates and the `{checkin}` / `{checkout}` template variables keep reading it unchanged.
  The end must be equal or after the start (validated in the form, the MCP and the import). Changing the mapping, or deleting or moving
  the mapped field, refreshes the copy of every lead. The pipeline card shows each mapped date with the label of its field.
- **Migration V019** (per company, applied with the module update): creates the sections table and `section_id`, relaxes the key check of
  `crm_field_definitions` to allow `fecha_ingreso` / `fecha_salida` (and `_2`, `_3`... when the key is taken), and, **only in companies that
  have leads with `target_start` or `target_end`**, creates the section "Datos de la reserva" with the date fields "Fecha de ingreso" (`fecha_ingreso`)
  and "Fecha de salida" (`fecha_salida`) (an existing date field with that key is reused), copies the dates into `custom_fields` (never overwriting a
  value already there) and sets the mapping. Companies without dates get nothing. The history of a date change now reads "Fecha de ingreso: antes -> despues".
- **Imports:** the columns for check-in/check-out (and the range column) write into the mapped fields; empty dates never erase. Without mapping
  the dates are ignored and the report of the row says so.
- **Agents:** `targetStart` and `targetEnd` of `crm_create_lead` / `crm_update_lead` are aliases that write in the mapped fields (error if none is
  chosen; send them in `customFields` instead if you prefer). `crm_list_pipeline` returns each field's `section` and `stayDates` (the labels of the
  mapped fields); `crm_get_lead` still returns `targetStart` / `targetEnd` (the copy) besides `customFields`.

## Sections for every field (V020, 0.23.0)

"Mover a Clientes" was removed (the 0.22.0 action, its permission check and code): a field such as `age` stays a custom field of the CRM.

- **Built-in sections are rows** of `crm_field_sections` with a `system_key` (V020, idempotent by key): `customer` "Datos del cliente" (name, email, phone,
  customer code, language; it used to be called "Contacto"), `followup` "Seguimiento", `message` "Mensaje y detalles" and `documents` "Documentos". V020 seeds them
  in today's order (positions 0..3) and moves the company's own sections after them, keeping their order. A custom section that already used a built-in name is renamed
  "... (propia)". Built-in sections keep their name and cannot be deleted (the screen hides the button and the server refuses); custom names cannot reuse theirs.
- **Any field in any section:** the field form lists every section (built-in and custom) plus "Campos personalizados" (no section). To use a new name, create the section first
  (it goes to the end) and then assign fields to it.
- **Order:** every section can move up or down (`settings:sectionMove`, `dir=up|down`, which renumbers positions), except **Datos del cliente**, which is always first (the code pins it
  whatever its stored position says; nothing can go before it).
- **How the form is built:** `leadFields` walks the sections in order; in each built-in section its built-in fields come first, then the custom fields assigned to it (by field position);
  the fields without section follow in "Campos personalizados". Required built-in fields keep their `required` flag wherever their section sits. The lead detail uses the same form.
- Agents: `crm_list_pipeline` returns `sections` (in order, `fixed` for built-in ones) and each field's `section`.

## Customer history (0.15.0)

When a lead moves to a won stage (from the board, a bulk action, a sold or booked item or an agent)
the CRM writes "Venta convertida en {empresa}" in the customer's history of the company, with the
interest, value, dates and items sold or booked, and a link that opens the lead
(`CrmCustomerEvents`, once per lead). The customer's requirements, documents, notes and stage
changes stay in the lead; the customer's history only lists the sale and the reservations of its
leads. Leads imported already won are not written.

The lead shows the customer's notes ("Notas del cliente", from Clientes: tastes and preferences
that apply to every lead) with a button that opens the customer's profile. The lead's own notes
("Notas del lead", in its activity) and "Detalles" are about that deal only.
